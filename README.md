# Research Compass

A free, student-friendly guide to academic research and scientific publishing.

## Features

- Research topic selection and literature searches
- Research methods and study design
- Research ethics and academic integrity
- Academic paper writing
- Electrical and Electronic Engineering research examples
- Journal selection and publication guidance
- Research glossary and useful tools
- Dark mode and adjustable text size
- Interactive checklists with locally saved progress

## Technologies

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
